Before you continue

Acknowledgment & disclaimer — please read before using this tool.

What this tool is

  • A free estimating tool that helps a producer or applicant gauge a person’s likely life-insurance health class before an application is submitted.
  • An estimate only — preliminary, non-binding, and based solely on the information entered.
  • Subject in every case to the carrier: each carrier has the final and absolute say on underwriting and health class.

What this tool is not

  • Not an offer of life insurance — not a quote, not a guarantee of coverage or price, and not an application.
  • It does not issue insurance, bind coverage, or take the place of a carrier underwriter’s decision.
  • It is not medical advice and not a medical diagnostic tool.
  • A favorable estimate does not mean any carrier will offer coverage — carriers may obtain medical records, prescription history, lab results, consumer reports, and MIB information that change the outcome.

Your privacy — answers stay on this device

  • Answers are saved automatically in this browser on this device. They are not sent to us, carriers or other services.
  • Every answer stays in this browser, on this device only. Your draft uses this browser’s local storage. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to view it.
  • There are no accounts and no answer tracking. Use Delete saved answers on the results page, or clear this site’s browser data, to remove your draft.
Legal disclaimer. This tool is not a medical diagnostic tool and does not issue insurance, bind coverage, or replace a carrier underwriter’s decision. The estimate is based only on disclosed information. The carrier may obtain medical records, prescription history, laboratory/paramedical results, consumer reports, and information from other insurers or MIB, subject to authorization — those sources can change the estimate. Never suggest withholding information or “answering around” a condition: applications state that answers influence acceptance and that material misrepresentation or nondisclosure can jeopardize coverage. Temporary coverage exists only if the exact carrier receipt conditions are met, not because this tool gives a favorable estimate.
